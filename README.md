## Hi, I'm Giuseppe Maset Neto (Neto)

Electrical Engineer (CREA-PR) and self-taught full-stack developer from Brazil. I build software that comes out of real field work: engineering calculation tools, inspection workflows and SaaS products for small businesses.

**Founder of [NEMAX Engenharia](https://nemaxengenharia.com.br)**: lightning protection (SPDA) per ABNT NBR 5419, electrical projects and EV charger infrastructure.

- **Engineering tools**: normative calculations encoded exactly as the standard defines them, with traceability and auditable manual overrides
- **SaaS**: [Sistema NEMAX](https://github.com/itsnetomaset/sistema-nemax), a multi-tenant platform for dental and health clinic management
- **Internal systems**: construction contract management, inspection checklists and operational panels
- **Websites** for local businesses
